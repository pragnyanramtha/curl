Rejected or deferred:

- Direct third-party skill installs remain deferred unless the user explicitly approves after skill-risk review.
- Vendoring `gstack` or large external frameworks remains deferred; local adaptations are enough.
- Standalone Product/PM, scope-guardian, communications, security, accessibility, DNS, cloud, mobile, or release-manager roles remain deferred because existing departments own those lenses.
- Full research folders, telemetry daemons, safety-hook frameworks, TDD-only policy, and self-improvement memory systems remain deferred until a concrete repeated failure proves the need.
- Additional review or release skills are deferred unless a real review miss, release miss, or routing miss appears.
